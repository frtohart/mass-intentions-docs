Privacy Policy for Mass Intentions Manager
Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. Overview
Mass Intentions Manager ("the App") is a local administrative tool designed to assist parish administrators and priests in recording, scheduling, and managing Holy Mass intentions. We prioritise user privacy and operate under a strict on-device data model.

2. Data Collection and Storage
• On-Device Storage: All recorded data—including parishioner names, Mass intention details, stipend amounts, and custom notes—is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple’s SwiftData framework.
• No Central Servers: We do not operate external databases, user accounts, or tracking servers. We cannot view, access, or sell your parish records.

3. App Permissions & System Integrations
The App requests specific iOS permissions solely to deliver core features:
• Apple Calendar (EventKit): Used exclusively to create and sync scheduled Masses to your chosen local calendar.
• Biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID): Used solely to authenticate app unlock on your local device. Biometric data is processed by iOS securely and is never accessible by the App.
• Files & Documents: Used when you explicitly perform a CSV import/export or link a shared iCloud Drive folder for local backup storage.
• iOS Widgets (WidgetKit): Uses on-device App Group storage to display today's scheduled Mass on your Lock Screen or Home Screen. No data is transmitted off your device or shared externally.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics
• Zero Third-Party Trackers: The App contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase), or cross-app tracking.
• Apple iCloud: If you enable iCloud sync or back up your device, your data is transmitted securely through Apple's end-to-end encrypted iCloud service subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Ownership & Retention
You maintain complete control and ownership of your data. You may delete individual records, perform local backups, or erase all database contents at any time via Settings -> Danger Zone within the App.
